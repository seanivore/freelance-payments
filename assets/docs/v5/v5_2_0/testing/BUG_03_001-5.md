## Bug_03_004: Homepage Pointer Tracking Glow Dim, Then Response Delay 

**EXPECTED BEHAVIOR**: 

  1. The glow effect on the homepage before the cursor haver over the form should be *almost* as bright as when the cursor is over the form. 
  2. When the cursor moves to hover over the form, the brightening of the glow effect is immediate. 

**ACTUAL BEHAVIOR**:

The glow effect is very dim before interaction with the form. When you hover over the form, you can see the state of the form change and a glow effect be added to the form. Then there is a delay, then the glow effect brightens up the cursor even brighter. It creates a jarring, staggard effect that does not look intentional. Ideally, the glow effect that reflects on the form elements, like the various input fields and the edges of the form, etc. should be effected independently of each other, and any one element being effected in one area, should have that area effected independent of the rest of the area of that element. As in, if the cursor is behind the right side of the top text field, the glow effect should reflect on the respective areas of the text field that is is behind and would reflect onto in a realistic, reflective way. The overall effect of the cursor glow is diminished by the way the entire form is effected, all over the form, at the same time, even when the cursor has not passed near all areas of the form element. For exceptional, reflective, realistic effect, the boarder around the form elements, like the stroke on a text input field, would look like clear glass allowing the light to reflect brighter through it when compared to the rest of the middle of the other elements where light looks "refracted" through it and thus not as bright. 

**IMAGES**:
